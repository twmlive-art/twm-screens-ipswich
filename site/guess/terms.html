Guess the Score: terms and conditions

Last updated 15 October 2026.

Promoter

Pasini Leisure Ltd, registered office 45 North Hill, Colchester CO1 1PY, trading as Three Wise Monkeys Ipswich, 22 Lloyds Avenue, Ipswich IP1 3HD ("TWM Ipswich").

Who can enter

Open to UK residents aged 18 or over. Staff of Pasini Leisure Ltd and their immediate families may not enter.

How to enter

Entry is free. No purchase is needed. Enter at the Guess the Score web page by giving your name, mobile number, email address and your prediction of the final score of the named home match. Entries for each home match open once the previous home match has kicked off and close at kick-off of the match in question. One entry per person per match. Entries made in more than one name, or with more than one mobile number or email address, will be removed.

To enter you must join Monkey Points, the Three Wise Monkeys loyalty scheme, and agree to receive marketing emails from Three Wise Monkeys. You can unsubscribe from marketing at any time using the link in any email; this does not affect any prize already won.

Winning

The final score means the score at the end of normal time including stoppage time, as published by the competition organiser. Extra time and penalties do not count. Every entry that matches the final score exactly wins. If a match is abandoned, postponed or played behind closed doors, entries carry over to the rearranged fixture where practical, or are void.

Prize

One free pint of Other Monkey Brewing draught beer (or a soft drink of equal value) at TWM Ipswich. Winners receive a code by email after the final whistle. The code is valid at TWM Ipswich only, on the day of the match, from the final whistle until close. It is not valid on any other day or at any other venue, has no cash value, cannot be transferred, extended or exchanged, and can be used once. Staff may ask for proof of age. The promoter may refuse to serve anyone under the Licensing Act 2003.

Your details

Your name, mobile number, email address and guess are stored by the promoter and shared with Square (the till and loyalty provider) to set up your Monkey Points account. They are used to run the competition, to run Monkey Points, and, with your consent, to send you marketing. We keep your entry details for 12 months after the match and then delete them; your Monkey Points account stays until you ask us to close it. See the Three Wise Monkeys privacy policy.

General

The promoter's decision on the result and on any dispute is final. The promoter may change, suspend or end the promotion at any time if required by law or by its premises licence. This promotion is not connected with or endorsed by any football club, league or broadcaster. The match is not shown at TWM Ipswich. The prize is not conditional on watching any sporting event, on the outcome of any event shown on the premises, or on the purchase of any drink. Prizes are served only in accordance with the premises licence and the Licensing Act 2003, and staff may refuse service at their discretion.
